## Simplifications in the animation (say so in a pinned comment or the description if asked)

- 5 pin stacks; real cylinders often have 5 or 6.
- Pins are shown as plain cylinders; real key pins usually have pointed or rounded tips.
- No security pins (spool/serrated) and no manufacturing tolerances.
- Cam shown as a simple bar at the back of the plug.
